# Translation review

## Scope

Reviewed the user-facing navigation, landing/about copy, pricing and FAQ text,
authentication labels, and the related technical wording in Uzbek and Russian.
The locale quality check also validates all 845 message keys in the `uz`, `ru`,
and `en` bundles.

## Changes made

- Replaced several literal translations of “finance engine” with the clearer
  “financial calculation system” wording in Uzbek and “financial calculation
  module” wording in Russian.
- Replaced mixed English “Desktop agent” wording in the Uzbek business-page
  copy with “Kompyuter agenti”.
- Improved Uzbek phrases that read literally, including the descriptions of
  moving from an idea to a well-grounded decision and of Pro planning.
- Used “Sarmoyador” for the Uzbek investor role labels.

## Review notes

- Uzbek copy uses Latin script and formal, direct instructions.
- Russian copy uses business-oriented wording and avoids the informal technical
  calque “движок” in reviewed financial explanations.
- FinAdvisor, product-tier names, currency codes, and platform names remain
  unchanged where they are official identifiers.
- This editorial pass is not a substitute for native-speaker proofreading.
  Legal and policy copy still requires the separate lawyer review tracked by
  H8 before publication.
